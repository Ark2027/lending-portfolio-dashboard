⚠
CRM Data Covers Only a Portion of Funded Loans

The majority of funded loans were originated through partner lender channels, not via the CRM pipeline. CRM-based comparisons (demand vs. deployment, state penetration rates) are directional signals only — they reflect pre-screen inquiries that entered the portal, not the full universe of borrowers served by partners. Do not treat CRM penetration rates as conversion metrics.
